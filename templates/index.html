🛡️ Neural Architecture

Multi-Branch Synthetic Media Verification

DeepTrust AI does not rely on simple pixel classifiers. It decomposes input media across four orthogonal forensic domains before passing through an attention gate into a binary classification head.

01

2D-FFT Frequency

Computes shifted Fast Fourier Transform magnitude maps. Generative models (GANs & Diffusion) leave distinct high-frequency checkerboard artifacts invisible to human perception.

Branch: SmallCNN(in_ch=1)
02

Structural Canny Edges

Extracts fine edge boundaries to detect blending seams, warping contours, and unnatural edge discontinuities around facial features and synthetic silhouettes.

Branch: ConvConvPool CNN
03

ResNet18 Semantic

Deep feature extractor generating 256-dimensional semantic representations of biological and object integrity, pupil reflections, and lighting coherence.

Branch: ResNet18 + Projection
04

Sensor Acquisition

Laplacian filtering isolates digital sensor camera noise. Real optical cameras introduce uniform sensor patterns, whereas AI models produce smoothed noise anomalies.

Branch: Laplacian SmallCNN
